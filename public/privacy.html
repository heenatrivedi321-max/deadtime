Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

The short, honest version: we built this to need as little of your data as possible. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking beyond what's required to bill sponsor impressions correctly. This page describes exactly what the code sends and stores — you can verify it yourself, the client is a short, readable script.

What we never collect

Your prompts, your code, or anything in your Claude Code conversation — it never leaves your machine.
Your name, IP-linked identity, or any account information — there is no account.
Browsing data, cookies, or third-party trackers on this website.

What we do collect — developer side

What we do collect — advertiser side

Company name, destination URL, the ad line text, and an email address, submitted directly through the advertiser form. Payment itself is handled entirely by PayPal — we never see or store your card or bank details; PayPal passes us only a confirmation that payment succeeded, along with PayPal's own order/capture/refund reference IDs, which we keep so a refund (see the Terms page) can actually be processed against the right payment.

Where it's stored

Everything lives in Cloudflare KV, a database attached to the Cloudflare Worker that runs this whole service. We don't run our own servers and don't copy this data anywhere else.

Third parties

  • PayPal — processes all payments and payouts. See PayPal's own privacy policy for how they handle your payment data.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and the database. Standard infrastructure logs (like any host) may briefly exist at that layer; we don't access or use them beyond debugging.
  • Google Fonts — this site's typefaces load from Google's servers, which is standard for most of the web but does mean Google sees the request (your IP address, which font, which page) the same way any font CDN would. No cookies, no account link, nothing beyond what loading any external resource inherently reveals. Self-hosting the fonts instead — removing this dependency entirely — is on the list, not done yet.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, for any reason.

Deleting your data

Since there's no account system, we can't offer a self-serve "delete my account" button. Email meanwhile@gmail.com with your install ID (or the payout/advertiser email you registered) and we'll remove your data from our database.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it ever changes, we'll update this page first.
